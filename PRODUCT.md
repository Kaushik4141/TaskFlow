# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

Windows and Linux desktop

## Users
You — a developer building multiple projects (TaskFlow, SkillForge, datavex3, Sakhi, Custom CMS), jumping between tools (Cursor, Jan, Obsidian, Notion, Firefox, Warp), researching LLM backends, and participating in hackathons. Someone whose work is fragmented across many windows, tabs, and tools, and who hates re-explaining to yourself what you were doing. Their context is in flow — switching between coding, researching, dictating, reading, planning. They don't want to stop and log what they're doing; they want the record to happen invisibly.

## Product Purpose
TaskFlow captures fragmented, multi-tool desktop activity into a coherent, searchable, linked personal knowledge base — without any manual effort during or after the session. Success means never losing context. The vault grows automatically, truthfully, and comprehensively so that past work can be queried and understood instantly.

## Positioning
Your work, auto-wiki'd. No logging. No setup. Just the next session.

## Brand Personality
Invisible. Faithful. Interlinked.
- **Invisible**: Runs in the background with zero friction (like Wispr Flow).
- **Faithful**: Records what actually happened truthfully.
- **Interlinked**: Everything connects (app → daily note → site → project → next action), paying off in Obsidian's graph view.
The feel is keyboard-first speed (Linear), deeply integrated AI (Cursor), zero-effort capture (Wispr Flow), and browsable history (Apple Time Machine).

## Anti-references
- **Notion**: Too much UI, too slow, form-heavy. TaskFlow should feel lighter than air.
- **Toggl / Clockify**: Manual start/stop timers. The whole point is no manual logging.
- **ActivityWatch**: Raw dashboards with cold charts and counters. TaskFlow tells stories, not just numbers.
- **Microsoft Recall**: Creepy, lacking transparency and user ownership. TaskFlow is a local vault entirely in the user's control.
- **Chat-style logs**: Plain text dumps with no structure, links, or graph.
- **Design patterns to avoid**: Modal dialogs for every action, pie charts/bar graphs, "Start/Stop capture" buttons, and cloud-first dependencies.

## Design Principles
- **Respect the Flow**: Interactions should never interrupt or demand attention from the user. Keyboard-first everything.
- **Narrative over Numbers**: Present data as interconnected stories and wiki-links, not sterile charts and counters.
- **Local Control, Deep AI**: The intelligence must feel deeply woven into the local, user-owned vault, never like a sidecar chatbot or a cloud service.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **Keyboard-first**: Every capture, config, and action must be fully accessible without a mouse.
- **Reduced motion**: Static background presence. No flashy UI, no unnecessary animations; system tray icons must be static.
- **High contrast indicators**: Status (recording, idle, error) uses shape plus color, not color alone.
- **Font agnostic & Screen reader compatible**: Clean markdown output without hardcoded fonts or confusing Unicode. Any native UI should use standard controls.
